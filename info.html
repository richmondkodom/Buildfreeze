WHY HYBRID SOLAR AIR CONDITIONER CAN SAVE ELECTRICITY
1. Solar collector panel aids energy reduction Compressor is the most electricity-consuming unit of air conditioner because it stays on almost throughout AC operation. A solar collector panel improves system temperature-pressure thus shuts off compressor to reduce energy consumption. Solar collector panels are much cheaper and are more efficient in converting solar energy to useful energy.
2. Utilises efficient heat-exchange system The use of the internal thread pipe, hydrophilic aluminum fin and the optimal heat exchange system reduce energy loss, improve the overall efficiency and effectively ensure the performance. Therefore, hybrid solar air conditioner is more convenient and energy-saving than regular air conditioner.
3. Less expensive Since the compressor is not always on, a smaller and energy saving compressor is a better choice used in the unit. This also contributes to a reduction in manufacturing and finished product cost.
4. Less noisy There is minimal noise from using the smaller compressor and no noise when compressor is off during solar operation. This contributes to a reduction in noise pollution indoors.
5. Durable and less maintenance cost The cost of maintaining the hybrid is minimal because the energy saving compressor is not always on; thus, takes long and cost less to replace. This hybrid type saves money.

MODE OF OPERATION HOW CONVENTIONAL AC WORKS Three major components ensure conditioning of room temperature: compressor, condenser and evaporator. The core to the cooling of an air conditioner is a refrigerant that moves in the air conditioning system. The refrigerant comes in as a cool low-pressure gas into the compressor, which uses electricity to compress it to a high-energy hot gas. The refrigerant then goes into the condenser where it dissipates all the heat and converts into very cold liquid under high pressure. It then goes into the evaporator fins where it exchanges heat with the drawn in room air (by the evaporator fan). The drawn-in air exchanges heat with the refrigerant in the evaporator. The cooled air is discharged back into the room. The process continuous till the desired temperature is attained, and then thermostat cuts off the whole unit. HOW HYBRID SOLAR AC WORKS The volatile refrigerant from the evaporator comes in as a cool low-pressure gas into the compressor, which uses electricity to compress it to a high-energy hot gas. The refrigerant then goes to exchange heat with the solar collector in order to increase its pressure-energy state. This principle is based on the pressure-temperature phenomenon: heated gases expands; thus, have high pressure. It enters condenser where it dissipates all the heat and converts into very cold liquid under high pressure. It then goes into the evaporator fins where it exchanges heat with the drawn in room air (by the evaporator fan). The drawn-in air exchanges heat with the refrigerant in the evaporator. The cooled air is discharged back into the room. The process continuous until the pressure in the system, created by the heat exchange in the solar collector, is appreciable to shut-off the compressor. The system pressure is maintained by the solar collector heat exchange until there is an appreciable drop, then the compressor turns on to restore system pressure. When the desired temperature is attained, then thermostat cuts off the whole unit. Any change in room temperature switches AC on and the cycle repeats.
